I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at IDSIA, part of the <a href="https://ir.inf.usi.ch/">IR Group</a> led by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EoGBsiMAAAAJ">Fabio Crestani</a>.  My research focuses on NLP for mental health, with a particular interest in how people express psychological states through language and how computational methods can help in better understanding and support well-being.

I have been lucky to work in interdisciplinary collaborations across Psychology and Digital Humanities, with the <a href="https://www.laboratorstiintecognitiveclinice.ro/">Laboratory for Clinical Cognitive Science</a> led by <a href="https://www.laboratorstiintecognitiveclinice.ro/ioana-podina/">Ioana Podină</a>, the <a href ="https://www.psycontrol-lab.de/">Psychiatric Control Systems Lab</a> led by <a href="https://www.psycontrol-lab.de/hamidreza"> Hamidreza Jamalabadi</a> and with the <a href="https://codhus.projects.uvt.ro/"> Codhus Research Centre</a> led by <a href="https://madalinachitez.com/">Mădălina Chitez</a>.

I received my PhD in October 2025 from the University of Bucharest, Romania and Universitat Politècnica de València, Spain under the supervision of <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=2SHcMNAAAAAJ">Liviu P. Dinu</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HFKXPH8AAAAJ">Paolo Rosso</a>. 

I am currently interested in how culture influences the manifestations of mental disorders and how we can develop culturally aware computational methods for more inclusive mental health screening.
